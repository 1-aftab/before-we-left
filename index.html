A note to the school

I don't really know how to put 15 years into a few paragraphs.

We spent so much of our lives here that school slowly became something we stopped noticing. The same classrooms, the same corridors, the same people, the same little things that felt completely normal at the time.

And then one day, it was suddenly the last day.

I think that's the strange part about growing up. You usually don't realize something was a memory until it's already over.

So this isn't really a goodbye letter. It's just a small place to keep some of the things that made those years what they were.

To the teachers who taught us, the friends who made ordinary days memorable, and everyone who was part of this chapter somehow — thank you.

We probably didn't realize it while we were here, but these were good years.

And I hope, wherever everyone ends up, we remember them that way.

— Someone from the class

Why this exists

Why I made this.

I've deleted photos before. Sometimes intentionally, sometimes accidentally. At the time it never felt like a big deal.

Then later, I'd wish I still had them.

After spending around 15 years in the same school, I realized I didn't actually have that many memories saved. Even the farewell photos I took ended up disappearing somehow.

So I made this little archive.

It isn't everything. It can't be.

It's just what survived, plus whatever everyone else is willing to share.

If you have photos from our school years or farewell that aren't here, send them in. Maybe we can slowly make this a little more complete.

Because I'd rather have an imperfect collection of memories than lose them completely.

End of the archive
